The new Ikshaa website is ready to look at. Here is where it has got to.

IT ANSWERS GUESTS ON ITS OWN

There is a chat assistant on every page. It handles the questions people

away temporary and fake addresses, so the list stays worth having, and every

A ROOM-BY-ROOM TOUR

 PART 2 — HOW TO LOOK ROUND YOUR WEBSITE
 (this can be sent on its own — written for Carman, on a phone)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Open your phone and go to:   ikshaa.pages.dev

Take about ten minutes. Go in this order.


 1.  THE FIRST PAGE

     The big photo changes by itself. Watch it for a moment.

     Now scroll down slowly. You will pass the rooms, the food, the village,
     and things to do nearby.

     Keep going to the bottom. That is the whole page.


 2.  THE MENU

     Look at the top left. Three small lines.

     Tap them. A list of ten pages slides down.

     Tap any name to go there.
     Tap the three lines again to close the list.


 3.  THE VILLA TOUR        (menu, then "Villa Tour")

     Thirty-five photos of the house, one after another.

     Swipe to go forward. Or do nothing, and it moves on its own.

     To leave, tap the small arrow at the top left.


 4.  THE GALLERY           (menu, then "Gallery")

     Nine boxes. One for the bedrooms, one for the pool, one for the
     courtyard, and so on.

     The photos inside each box change by themselves.

     Tap a box to see them big, then swipe left and right.
     Tap the X to close.


 5.  THE CHAT              (round button, bottom right of every page)

     This is the part to spend time on.

     Tap the round button. Type a question the way you would say it out loud.

     Try these three:

         how many people can stay
         is there hot water
         how far is the airport

     Now try one it cannot know:

         how much does it cost

     It should tell you it does not know, and give your email address.
     It should NOT make up a price.

     That is the most important thing to check. A guest trusts a website that
     admits what it does not know. Please try a few questions of your own,
     especially awkward ones.


 6.  THE JOURNAL           (menu, then "The Balcao")

     Four pieces of writing about Goa: the food, the old houses, the rains.

     There is a list at the top. Tap a title to jump to it.


 7.  THE QUESTIONS PAGE    (menu, then "Questions")

     Seventeen questions guests ask often.

     Tap any question and the answer opens underneath.


 8.  THE LETTERS           (bottom of the first page, "Subscribe")

     Put in your own email and send it.

     A welcome note should reach you in about a minute. Look in your spam
     folder too, and tell me if it lands there — that is worth knowing.

     At the bottom of that note there is a link to stop the letters. It works.


 9.  BOOK NOW              (top right of every page)

     Takes a guest to the booking page, and from there to Airbnb.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG

 Tell me two things:

     which page you were on
     what you saw

 A photo in the wrong place, a word spelled wrong, an answer that reads
 oddly — all of it is quick to change. Nothing is too small to mention.

 PART 3 — WHAT WE NEED FROM CARMAN
===============================================================================

 Grouped by how much difference each one makes. If she answers only the first
 group, that is most of the value.


 FIRST — the questions guests ask before they book
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   [ ]  RATES
        Weekday, weekend and peak — or confirm "the listing only, always",
        which is a perfectly good answer and the site will say so.

   [ ]  MINIMUM STAY
        Any, and whether it changes over Christmas and New Year.

   [ ]  CANCELLATION TERMS
        What happens, and by when.

   [ ]  DEPOSIT
        The site says 50% to confirm, non-refundable. Is that still right?
        And the refundable security deposit — how much?

   [ ]  PAYMENT METHODS
        Bank transfer, card, UPI, cash on arrival?

   [ ]  HOUSE RULES — the four that decide bookings
        Pets       — allowed? any size or breed limit?
        Smoking    — anywhere, outside only, or not at all?
        Parties    — where is the line between a family dinner and an event?
        Children   — welcome? cot available? any age limits?

   [ ]  A PHONE OR WHATSAPP NUMBER
        The site offers only email at the moment. For a villa booking a
        number does far better — most enquiries in India begin on WhatsApp.


 SECOND — asked often, currently unanswered
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   [ ]  CAMERAS AND PERIMETER SECURITY
        Worth handling carefully. A guest asking whether they are recorded is
        asking about their privacy and deserves a straight answer. The site
        refuses to guess, which is right — but a real answer is better than a
        refusal. Are there cameras, where are they, and is anyone on the
        property overnight?

   [ ]  ACCESSIBILITY
        Steps, doorways, bathrooms. Described honestly rather than in general
        terms — somebody is planning around it.

   [ ]  PARKING
        How many cars, and whether it is inside the gate.

   [ ]  HOT WATER
        Every bathroom, around the clock?

   [ ]  MOSQUITO NETS AND REPELLENT
        Provided, or should guests bring their own?

   [ ]  EV CHARGING
        Increasingly asked. Is there a point at the house, or nearby?

   [ ]  TOUR GUIDE AND CATERING
        Can either be arranged, and roughly what does it cost?


 THIRD — two things I am stuck on
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   [ ]  THE BEACH DRIVE
        The Airbnb listing says "a 20 mins drive away from the Goa airport or
        from the nearest beaches of south Goa". The website says fifteen
        minutes to the beaches, in sixteen separate places. Which is right?
        I have left it alone rather than change all sixteen on a guess.

   [ ]  A DOMAIN NAME
        Everything currently sits at ikshaa.pages.dev. Search reputation
        builds up on a domain and starts again from nothing when you move, so
        the sooner this is settled the better. ikshaa.com, or similar.

        This is also what the newsletter is waiting for. Nobody subscribes to
        a site they cannot find.


 ONE TO RAISE IN CONVERSATION, NOT IN WRITING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   The Airbnb rating is strong — 4.93 from 165 reviews, Superhost, replies
   within the hour. That belongs on the site near the booking button, where
   it will do more for enquiries than another photograph would.

   Two conditions. Ask her permission first. And it should appear as plain
   text crediting Airbnb, not marked up for Google as the site's own rating —
   Google penalises sites that present reviews collected elsewhere as theirs,
   and the penalty applies to the whole domain, not just the page.
