Hi Carman,

The new Ikshaa website is live and I wanted to show you where it has got to.

    ikshaa.pages.dev

A few things it does that an ordinary villa site does not.

IT ANSWERS GUESTS ON ITS OWN
There is a chat assistant on every page that handles the questions people
actually ask — the pool, breakfast, how to get here from either airport,
arriving by train, what is included, how many it sleeps. It covers around
forty-four topics.

It is written so that it will never invent an answer. If it does not know
something it says so plainly and points the guest to your email, rather than
guessing and having them plan around something untrue. It costs nothing per
message, however many people use it.

A WEEKLY LETTER
Guests can subscribe and get a short note from the house. Five people have
signed up already. The form turns away temporary and fake addresses, so the
list stays worth having, and every letter carries a one-click unsubscribe.

A JOURNAL
Four pieces about Goa — what susegad actually means, what the food really is,
the great houses at Loutolim and Chandor, and why the monsoon is worth coming
for. It gives people a reason to stay on the site, and it is the sort of
writing that helps Google find you.

A ROOM-BY-ROOM TOUR
Thirty-five photographs you can walk through, and a gallery of another
thirty-seven sorted by room — bedrooms, bathrooms, the courtyard, the pool,
the grounds.

IT WORKS PROPERLY ON A PHONE
Which is where most people will see it.

IT IS BUILT TO BE FOUND
Set up so Google reads it correctly, and so do the AI assistants people
increasingly use to plan travel. The site tells those assistants, in writing,
which things they must not guess at — your rates, your check-in times,
whether there are cameras — so they point to you instead of inventing a
figure.

IT COSTS ALMOST NOTHING TO RUN
The hosting, the database and the email all sit inside free allowances.

I have matched every detail to your Airbnb listing — three bedrooms, four
beds, two bathrooms, up to nine guests, check-in from 2pm, check-out by 11am
— and put your Goa registration number, HOTS000253, in the footer of every
page.

There is a short list below of things only you can answer. Once I have them,
several parts of the site stop saying "ask the owner" and start giving guests
a straight answer instead.

HOW TO LOOK ROUND IT

Open ikshaa.pages.dev on your phone. Then:

  1. THE FRONT PAGE
     The photographs change on their own. Scroll down slowly and you will
     pass the rooms, the food, the village, things to do, and the signup for
     the letters at the bottom.

  2. THE MENU
     Tap the three lines at the top left. Ten pages. Tap any one to go
     there. Tap the three lines again to close it.

  3. THE VILLA TOUR      (menu, then Villa Tour)
     Thirty-five photographs, one at a time. Swipe to move, or wait and it
     moves by itself. The arrow at the top left brings you back.

  4. THE GALLERY         (menu, then Gallery)
     Nine boxes, one for each part of the house. The photographs inside each
     box change on their own. Tap a box to see them full size, then swipe
     left and right.

  5. THE CHAT
     The round button at the bottom right of every page. Tap it and ask
     anything the way you would say it out loud. Try:
         how many people can stay
         is there hot water
         how far is the airport
     Then try asking something it cannot know, such as the price. It should
     say it does not know and give your email address. That part is the one
     worth testing, because it is what stops it telling a guest something
     untrue.

  6. THE JOURNAL         (menu, then The Balcao)
     Four pieces of writing about Goa. Tap a title in the list at the top to
     jump straight to it.

  7. THE QUESTIONS PAGE  (menu, then Questions)
     Seventeen common questions. Tap one to open the answer.

  8. THE LETTERS         (bottom of the front page, Subscribe)
     Try it with your own address. A welcome note should arrive within a
     minute, with an unsubscribe link at the bottom of it.

  9. BOOK NOW
     Top right of every page. It goes to the booking page, and from there to
     Airbnb.

If anything looks wrong, or reads wrong, just tell me which page and what
you saw. Small corrections are quick.

 PART 2 — WHAT WE NEED FROM CARMAN
===============================================================================

 Grouped by how much difference each one makes. If she only answers the first
 group, that is most of the value.


 FIRST — the questions guests ask before they book
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   [ ]  RATES
        Weekday, weekend and peak — or confirm "the listing only, always",
        which is a perfectly good answer and the site will say so.

   [ ]  MINIMUM STAY
        Any, and does it change over Christmas and New Year.

   [ ]  CANCELLATION TERMS
        What happens, and by when.

   [ ]  DEPOSIT
        The site says 50% to confirm, non-refundable. Is that still right?
        And the refundable security deposit — how much?

   [ ]  PAYMENT METHODS
        Bank transfer, card, UPI, cash on arrival?

   [ ]  HOUSE RULES — the four that decide bookings
        Pets       — allowed? any size or breed limit?
        Smoking    — anywhere, outside only, or not at all?
        Parties    — where is the line between a family dinner and an event?
        Children   — welcome? cot available? any age limits?

   [ ]  A PHONE OR WHATSAPP NUMBER
        The site currently offers only email. For a villa booking, a number
        converts far better — most enquiries in India start on WhatsApp.


 SECOND — asked often, currently unanswered
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   [ ]  CAMERAS AND PERIMETER SECURITY
        Worth handling carefully. A guest asking whether they are recorded is
        asking a privacy question and deserves a straight answer. The site
        currently refuses to guess, which is right — but a real answer is
        better than a refusal. Are there cameras, where are they, and is
        anyone on the property overnight?

   [ ]  ACCESSIBILITY
        Steps, doorways, bathrooms. Described honestly rather than
        generally — somebody is planning around it.

   [ ]  PARKING
        How many cars, and is it inside the gate.

   [ ]  HOT WATER
        Every bathroom, around the clock?

   [ ]  MOSQUITO NETS AND REPELLENT
        Provided, or should guests bring their own?

   [ ]  EV CHARGING
        Increasingly asked. Is there a point, or somewhere nearby?

   [ ]  TOUR GUIDE AND CATERING
        Can either be arranged, and roughly what do they cost?


 THIRD — two things I am stuck on
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   [ ]  THE BEACH DRIVE
        The Airbnb listing says "a 20 mins drive away from the Goa airport or
        from the nearest beaches of south Goa". The website says fifteen
        minutes to the beaches, in sixteen different places. Which is right?
        I have left it alone rather than change all sixteen on a guess.

   [ ]  A DOMAIN NAME
        Everything currently lives at ikshaa.pages.dev. Search reputation
        builds up on a domain and starts again from nothing when you move, so
        the sooner this is decided the better. ikshaa.com or similar.


 ONE TO RAISE IN CONVERSATION, NOT IN WRITING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   The Airbnb rating is strong — 4.93 from 165 reviews, Superhost, replies
   within the hour. That belongs on the site, near the booking button, where
   it will do more for enquiries than another photograph.

   Two conditions. Ask her permission first. And it should appear as plain
   text crediting Airbnb, not marked up for Google as the site's own rating —
   Google penalises sites that present reviews collected elsewhere as theirs,
   and the penalty applies to the whole domain, not just the page.
