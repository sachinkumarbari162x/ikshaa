Finding Us

Scroll

The house sits back from the road, in a village that rewards arriving slowly.

Ikshaa is in Loutolim, in the Salcete taluka of South Goa — inland from the coast, among red earth, coconut groves and old village houses. It is one of Goa’s prettiest villages, with narrow winding roads and impressive heritage mansions. The turning is quiet and easy to pass; the map below is the surest way to find it.

The pin currently marks the village of Loutolim rather than the gate — see the note below about setting it exactly.

Airport transfers are complimentary — tell us your flight and we will arrange the car.

Arriving

Taxis are straightforward from either airport and from Madgaon, and drivers know Loutolim well. If you are driving yourself, the village roads narrow in places and are best taken gently — particularly during the monsoon, when the verges soften.

Tell us when you expect to arrive and we will make sure someone is at the house to meet you.
