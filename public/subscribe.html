Keeping in touch

What arrives, and how often

A house this old keeps its own calendar. The light moves, the rains come and go, the garden turns over. We write when there is something worth telling you about.

  • 01

    One letter a week

    Short, and from the house itself — what is flowering, what the cook is making, what the weather has been doing to the courtyard.

  • 02

    A note when the season turns

    The monsoon breaking, the Carnival, the quiet weeks after Christmas. Only when Goa genuinely changes — a handful of times a year.

  • 03

    Nothing else

    No offers, no discounts, no campaigns. We do not sell or pass on your address, and one click at the foot of any letter ends it.
