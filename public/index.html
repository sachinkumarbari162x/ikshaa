This is where Ikshaa begins

A heritage villa with a private pool, set in a South Goan village that has kept its own pace — twenty minutes from the airport, and a world away from it.

Welcome to Ikshaa

A hidalgo’s villa? A mansion? A temple?

Ikshaa in pristine Loutolim is a harmonious blend of all of these — the ultimate heritage luxury villa in Goa for people seeking refuge from big-city stress. It has the comforts of modern living, but all the earthy charm of the Goa of yesteryears, down to the little recesses in the walls for the old ponttios, or paraffin lamps.
